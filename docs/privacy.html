Privacy Policy for REMI Discord Bot

Last Updated: November 18, 2026

Thank you for using REMI ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you interact with the Bot on Discord.

By inviting or using REMI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Bot.

1. Information We Collect

REMI collects the minimum amount of data necessary to provide conversational AI functionality:

  • Discord User ID: A unique numerical identifier provided by Discord, used to associate conversation context with your user account.
  • Interaction Message Content: Prompts and messages that you explicitly direct to REMI (such as messages mentioning the bot, replying to the bot, or commands like !ask).
  • Message Timestamps & Roles: Identifying whether a message was sent by the user or the bot to maintain chronological conversation memory.
What We DO NOT Collect:
  • We do NOT read or store messages in channels where the bot is not directly addressed or mentioned.
  • We do NOT collect personal real-world identities, real names, email addresses, physical locations, or phone numbers.
  • We do NOT collect passwords, payment information, or voice audio data.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used strictly for:

  • Contextual AI Conversations: Maintaining short-term memory (up to the last 20 messages) so the AI understands ongoing conversational threads.
  • Query Processing: Transmitting the conversation prompt to the Google Gemini API to generate responses.
  • Service Stability & Troubleshooting: Ensuring uptime and debugging unexpected application errors.

We never sell, lease, rent, or monetize your data. We do not use your data for commercial advertising, targeted tracking, or user profiling.

3. Data Storage, Retention, and Security

  • Storage: Data is stored in a secure, authenticated PostgreSQL database.
  • Strict Retention Limit: We enforce an automated rolling buffer: only the 20 most recent messages per user are retained. Once this limit is exceeded, older messages are permanently and automatically deleted.
  • Security Measures: We employ standard industry safeguards, including SSL/TLS encrypted database connections and access controls to prevent unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services

In order to provide our service, REMI interfaces with the following third parties:

  • Discord API (Discord Inc.): Facilitates bot messaging and user interactions. For more details, see Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC): Used as our Large Language Model (LLM) backend to generate responses. User queries are handled under Google's enterprise Generative AI terms and data protection policies.

5. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your stored data:

  • Instant Self-Service Deletion: You can completely clear your conversation history from our database at any time by sending the command !clear (or !cleardata) in any channel or direct message with the bot.
  • Manual Erasure Request: You may also contact the developer to request manual deletion of all records associated with your Discord User ID. Such requests will be completed within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

REMI does not knowingly collect or solicit data from children under 13 years of age (or the legal age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). If you believe a minor has provided information to the bot, please contact us for immediate data removal.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to revise this Privacy Policy at any time. Any revisions will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this document. Continued interaction with the Bot signifies your acceptance of any revisions.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or privacy requests regarding REMI, please reach out via:

  • GitHub Repository: https://github.com/just-kett/REMI
  • Developer Contact: Contact via Discord or open an issue on the GitHub repository.
